# Instagram Algorithm Playbook

> How Instagram actually ranks and distributes content, translated into plain, buildable rules.
> Source of truth: Instagram's own public statements (creator blog, Adam Mosseri's posts/AMAs, the
> "how Instagram ranking works" transparency articles). Where a rule is a community-observed heuristic
> rather than a stated fact, it is marked **[heuristic]**.

## The one idea that fixes most mistakes

There is **no single "the algorithm."** Feed, Stories, Reels, and Explore each rank content
differently because each surface has a different job:

| Surface | Primarily serves | Optimizes for | Your job here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | People who already follow you | Recency + relationship | Deepen relationship, drive profile/saves |
| **Stories** | Existing followers | Relationship + retention | Daily presence, replies, polls |
| **Reels** | **Strangers** | Entertainment + watch time | **Reach / new followers** |
| **Explore** | Strangers | Discovery + interest match | Reach into new interest graphs |

**Followers see your Stories; strangers meet you in Reels.** Post accordingly: **Stories for
retention, Reels for reach.** Most accounts do the reverse — polished "reach" content to existing
followers, throwaway content to strangers — and then wonder why nothing grows.

## Ranking signals (rough priority order, per Instagram's stated emphasis)

1. **Watch time / completion** — did people finish it? (dominant on Reels)
2. **Likes**
3. **Sends (shares to DM)** — see below; the most under-used lever
4. **Comments**
5. **Profile visits after viewing** — a strong "who is this?" intent signal

### Sends are the heavy signal

Instagram has openly said **sends-per-reach** is a signal they watch closely.
- A **save** says "useful to *me*."
- A **send** says "I vouched for this to a *friend*." It's rarer and weighs more.

So the highest-leverage question for any draft is: **"Would a specific person send this to a
specific friend?"** Content that earns sends:
- Relatable, specific struggles ("this is so us")
- Useful checklists / saveable how-tos
- In-group humor
Content that does **not** earn sends: generic motivation, vague inspiration, "brand" posts with no
person on the other end.

## Reels: the audition model

Instagram gives new/smaller accounts **test distribution**: your Reel is shown to a **small batch of
strangers**, and its performance there earns the next, larger batch. The "small account = invisible"
excuse is backwards on Reels — small accounts get **auditions constantly**. Most fail the audition in
**the first second**.

**The first second decides everything**, because the swipe decision happens instantly.
- **Cold-open into the payoff promise.** Trim every warm-up frame ("Hey guys, so today…").
- Make the first frame state or show the reason to stay.

## Originality is explicitly favored

Instagram has said it **downranks visibly reposted content** (watermarks from other apps like
TikTok, recycled clips) and **boosts original creation**, especially for smaller accounts.
- Post original, natively-created content.
- Remove other-platform watermarks; don't recycle obviously-lifted clips.

## The boring mechanics they've confirmed

- **Hashtags** help categorization but are a **minor** signal: **3–5 relevant beats 30 spammy.**
- **Early engagement matters** — reply to comments in the first hour; **comments beget comments.**
- **Consistency** builds relationship signals with followers, which feeds your **Feed** ranking.
- You are **not** punished for skipping a feature. Instagram has repeatedly said this. But **active
  accounts naturally generate more signals**, so activity correlates with reach without being a
  direct "use everything or else" rule.

## Judge success by the right metric

**Judge by sends and watch time, not likes.** Likes are a vanity lagging indicator; sends and
completion are what the ranker actually rewards and what predicts reach on the next post.

## Strategy, rebuilt from Instagram's own statements

- **Reels:** original, **cold-open**, built to be **SENT**, 3–5 hashtags. This is your reach engine.
- **Stories:** daily-ish, for people who already follow you; **polls and replies deepen the
  relationship signal** that lifts your Feed ranking.
- **First hour:** live in your comments — early engagement compounds.
- **Scoreboard:** sends and watch time first; followers gained second; likes last.

## Do this / avoid this

**Do**
- Open Reels on the payoff; earn the first second.
- Write every caption/hook toward "who would send this to whom?"
- Post original, watermark-free content.
- Reply to every early comment in the first hour.
- Use Stories for retention (polls, questions, replies), Reels for reach.
- Keep hashtags to 3–5 relevant tags.

**Avoid**
- Warm-up intros and slow builds on Reels.
- Reposted / other-app-watermarked clips.
- Generic motivation with no sendable specificity.
- 20–30 hashtag walls.
- Measuring the post by likes.

## For BettorEdge specifically (edit to taste)

- **Reels = reach:** cold-open on a *result or a hot take* ("This $20 bet paid $340 — here's the
  read"), not "Hey bettors, welcome back." Built to be **sent** to a buddy who'd say "we should tail
  this."
- **Sendable formats:** the saveable slate breakdown, the "this is so us" bad-beat humor, the
  quick edge-checklist.
- **Stories = the community:** daily slate polls ("who you got tonight?"), reply to picks, run the
  relationship signal that keeps the marketplace sticky.
- **Originality:** native graphics of *your* marketplace odds/bets beat reposted sportsbook screenshots.
