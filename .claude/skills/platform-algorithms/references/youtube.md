# YouTube Algorithm Playbook

> How YouTube ranks and distributes video for a brand/creator maximizing reach.
> Anchored on YouTube/Google statements and Help docs; creator-analyst consensus is marked **[heuristic]**.
> **Last verified:** 2026-07.

## The one idea

YouTube's system is **pull, not push**: *"The algorithm doesn't look for viewers for your content; it
looks for content for specific viewers."* It does not "promote channels" — it matches a **video +
packaging** to an individual viewer it thinks will be satisfied. You're not competing for a slot;
you're **auditioning to a viewer**. Everything below is winning that audition and proving satisfaction
after the click.

## Surfaces — who each serves

| Surface | Serves | Optimizes for |
|---|---|---|
| **Home / Browse** | Mostly strangers + light subs | Long-term interest match. Usually the **biggest** traffic source; open to small channels. |
| **Suggested / Up-next** | Mix, tilts to discovery | "What you'll watch *next*" — biggest session-extension + new-audience lever |
| **Search** | Intent (subs + strangers) | Relevance + engagement/retention. The **evergreen / long-tail** engine (ranks for months/years) |
| **Shorts feed** | Overwhelmingly strangers | Swipe economics — **separate ranking system**; subs matter far less |
| **Subscriptions / Notifications** | Existing subscribers | New-upload delivery; drives first-hour velocity |

Discovery = Home + Suggested + Shorts. Loyalty = Subscriptions + Notifications. **Subscriber count is
a weak reach guarantee** — most views come from Browse/Suggested to non-subscribers (the pull model).
Note: YouTube **removed the Trending page (July 2025)** — every feed is now individualized.

## Ranking signals

YouTube's stated stack: **clicks → watch time → survey responses → sharing → likes → dislikes**, with
a growing, heavily-weighted **satisfaction** layer on top, and signals weighted **differently by
context** (watch time weighs more on TV than on mobile). YouTube optimizes for **"valued watchtime"** —
time the *viewer* considers well-spent, measured partly via **1–5 satisfaction surveys**.
(Analysts frame 2025–2026 as "satisfaction-weighted discovery"; the safely-official version is that
satisfaction is a heavy layer on top of engagement, not a replacement. **[heuristic]**)

**Long-form priority (Home / Suggested / Search):**
1. **CTR on impressions** (thumbnail + title) — gets you into the audition.
2. **Average view duration / % retention** — especially the **first 30 seconds**.
3. **Satisfaction** — surveys, return visits within ~7 days, shares, "not interested" suppression.
4. **Session contribution** — does your video keep the viewer *on YouTube* afterward. **[heuristic]**
5. **Personalization fit**, then **freshness/velocity** (matters more for news than evergreen).
6. Likes/comments/shares — real but *lower-weighted* confirmatory signals.

**Shorts priority (separate system):**
1. **Viewed vs. swiped-away** — the hook score (stayed past the first ~2s?).
2. **Average % viewed.**
3. **Loops / replays** — since **Mar 31 2025** each loop counts as another view; >100% avg % viewed is one of the strongest positives.
4. Engagement + re-watch.
**CTR is NOT a Shorts signal** — there's no thumbnail; users swipe. Biggest mechanical difference from long-form.

## The audition (long-form)

Distribution is gated in sequence — each stage unlocks the next:
**Impressions → CTR → Retention → Satisfaction → more impressions.**
- An impression counts when your thumbnail is ≥50% visible for ≥1s; shown to a small test pool first.
- **The trap:** high CTR + weak retention *loses* to lower CTR + strong retention. A clicked-a-lot
  video that drops viewers in the first 15–30s gets demoted ("Quality CTR" **[heuristic]**). The
  thumbnail is a **promise**; the first 30 seconds must **pay it off.**
- CTR is context-relative (4% from Browse ≠ 4% from Search) — never chase a raw number across sources.
  Test thumbnail+title as a **package** via **Test & Compare** (~2,000+ impressions/variant).

## Shorts vs. long-form

- Separate systems. Shorts view counts inflate (any playback counts since Mar 2025) — judge Shorts by
  **avg % viewed, swipe-away, loops**, not view count.
- **Shorts subscribers convert poorly** to long-form (widely cited <0.2% natural crossover) — a Shorts
  sub is a low-intent sub. The **highest-value move** is a viewer clicking *from a Short to a long-form
  video on the same channel*; attach a **Related-video link** to each Short. **[heuristic]**
- **Shorts = reach (top of funnel); long-form = depth, subscribers, revenue.** Don't judge them on the
  same metric.

## Downranked / penalized

- **Misleading thumbnails/titles** (malicious clickbait) — removable/age-restrict/strike; Google's
  clickbait crackdown may **remove the video without a strike** to let you adjust.
- **"Quality CTR" demotion** — honest-but-weak openings suppressed even without a violation. **[heuristic]**
- **Inauthentic content** (renamed Jul 2025) — mass-produced/templated/low-substance = ineligible for monetization.
- **Reused content** — clips/compilations/reactions monetize only with significant original commentary/editing.
- **Spam / engagement manipulation** / watch-time gaming — monetization suspension or termination.
- **Negative feedback** ("Not interested", "Don't recommend channel", low surveys) suppresses future recs.

## Concrete tactics

- **Packaging:** design thumbnail + title as one promise the first 30s keeps; A/B test; judge against retention, not CTR alone.
- **Hook (first 15s):** state the payoff/stakes ("by the end you'll…", a concrete before/after number);
  pattern interrupt in the first 5s; target **80%+** retention through the first 30s.
- **Retention edit:** front-load tight pacing, flatten the curve, re-hook before predictable drop points.
- **Chapters:** let viewers self-navigate to value (raises watch time; wins Google "key moments").
- **End screens:** last 5–20s; best layout = **one video element + one subscribe element** (not the 4-tile grid); chains sessions.
- **Cadence:** consistency > raw frequency; there's **no official "post daily" reward** — a schedule you can sustain that gives the system fresh at-bats.
- **Shorts→long funnel:** attach a Related-video link; keep Shorts topically adjacent to your long-form. Treat the crossover click as a key KPI.

**Which metric matters most:** **fix retention (first-30s AVD %) first, packaging (CTR, in context) second, everything else third.** High CTR on weak retention is the classic self-inflicted demotion.

## Do this / avoid this

**Do**
- Win the packaging audition, then retention-edit for a flat curve.
- Nail the first 15–30s; use chapters and a 1+1 end screen.
- Use Shorts as discovery top-of-funnel with a Related-video link to long-form.
- Judge long-form by retention + satisfaction + session, not raw views/subs.

**Avoid**
- Misleading thumbnails/titles; optimizing CTR in isolation; comparing CTR across traffic sources.
- Judging Shorts by view count; expecting Shorts subs to convert on their own.
- Mass-produced/templated uploads; gaming engagement; chasing a magic upload frequency over quality.
