# Threads (Meta) Algorithm Playbook

> How Threads ranks and distributes posts, for a brand/creator maximizing reach.
> Anchored on Adam Mosseri's public statements and Meta newsroom posts; community-observed items are
> marked **[heuristic]**.
> **Last verified:** 2026-07.

## The one idea

Threads is a **conversation-optimizing, For-You-first** network, and its most load-bearing official
advice is Mosseri's: **"If you're really trying to grow, you should reply much more than you post.
The sum of all your replies is about as valuable as the sum of all your posts."** Because For You is
the default *and* surfaces non-followers heavily, a small account still gets real discovery — Threads'
biggest structural edge over X.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Ordering | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| **For You** (default) | Algorithmic | Followed **+ unconnected** content → primary discovery engine |
| **Following** | Reverse-chronological (no algorithm) | Your followers only |
| **Search** — incl. **"Recent" tab** | Ranked + a chronological Recent tab | Strangers with intent; timely posts surface in Recent |
| **Topic tags / Communities** | Interest-clustered | Topic-based discovery (one topic tag per post; Communities launched Oct 2025) |

Meta **rebalanced in Nov 2024** toward followed accounts, so unconnected reach tightened somewhat and
connected reach rose — but strangers are still a first-class part of For You.

## Ranking signals (rough priority)

1. **Replies & conversation depth** — the single most emphasized signal. Substantive back-and-forth ≫ likes.
2. **Reposts** — strongest amplification signal.
3. **Predicted engagement / relevance to the viewer** — Meta's ML predicts whether *you'll* interact.
4. **Early engagement velocity** — quality replies in the first ~30–60 min. **[heuristic]**
5. **Likes** — counted, weighted below replies/reposts.
6. **Recency** — For You favors fresher content; Following + Search-Recent are chronological.
7. **Originality / quality** — original, guideline-compliant posts favored; novel perspectives get extra exposure.

**Changes to internalize:** engagement-bait is being actively down-ranked (Mosseri, Oct 2024);
**links now perform better** after a 2025 ranking optimization (reversing the old link penalty) — but
link-*only* posts still have the lowest engagement, so pair links with native content or drop in a
reply. Political/borderline content is **not recommended to non-followers by default** (softening from
2025 but still viewer-controllable) — treat it as reach-limited. Guideline violators lose eligibility
to be recommended (check the **Account Status** dashboard).

## Format: what wins

Engagement rates run (Buffer, 2026): **Video > Images > Text > link-only.** Carousels (from Instagram)
often top the list when each slide has a caption. So: **lead with a strong text hook, then pair it
with an image, carousel, or short video.** Link-only posts are weakest.

**Replies are a format, not an afterthought:**
- **Self-reply / reply-ladder:** continue your own thread in the replies to add depth and restart velocity.
- **Reply to others** (esp. larger niche accounts) within the first hour — your reply can itself be
  surfaced, and it's the top follower-acquisition move. Reply *more than you post.*

## First hour

The first ~30–60 min set a reach ceiling: strong early reply/repost velocity from a small test
audience triggers wider For-You distribution; a slow start caps the post. **Quality** of early replies
matters — the system discounts "nice!"/emoji-only comments. Seed 1–2 genuine replies within ~10 min
and be present to answer real commenters for the first hour. **[heuristic]**

## Downranked / penalized

- **Engagement bait** ("like if…", "follow for…", "comment X") — explicitly targeted.
- **Link-only posts** — best to place links in a reply or pair with native content.
- **Reposted / repetitive / spammy** content.
- **Political / borderline** content — reach-limited to non-followers by default.
- **Guideline violations** — remove recommendation eligibility.

## Instagram interoperability (a core advantage)

A Threads account **is** your Instagram login; existing IG followers can follow you on Threads in one
tap (you don't start from zero), the profile links to IG, and you can cross-post Threads → Instagram.
There is **no native Threads↔X integration** — adapt voice per platform, don't copy-paste from X.

## Judge success by

1. **Replies / conversation depth** — most aligned with the ranker and best leading indicator of reach.
2. **Reposts** — best amplification signal.
3. **Followers gained** — especially from *your replies* on others' posts. Reply-driven brands out-gain post-only brands.
Likes and impressions are vanity/lagging. Watch **Account Status** to stay recommendable.

## Do this / avoid this

**Do**
- Reply more than you post; reply to bigger niche accounts within the first hour.
- Lead with a hook + a genuine question/take; pair with image/carousel/video.
- Seed real conversation in the first 10–60 min; use self-replies to add depth.
- Add one topic tag; use Search "Recent" for timely topics; leverage IG follower carryover + cross-post to IG.

**Avoid**
- "Like/follow/comment-if" bait and emoji-only reply farming.
- Link-only posts. Reposting unoriginal content as your main output.
- Leaning on political/borderline content for reach. Copy-pasting X voice.

## For BettorEdge specifically (edit to taste)

- Threads is casual and conversation-first — post a genuine question a stranger can answer without
  context ("What's the one bet you're actually confident in tonight?"), then **run the reply ladder**.
- Your biggest lever is **replying into larger sports/betting accounts within the hour** — that's the
  discovery + follower-acquisition channel here, and it compounds off your IG following carryover.
- Pair takes with a native carousel (slate breakdown, one edge per slide). Keep the marketplace link
  in a reply, not the post body.
