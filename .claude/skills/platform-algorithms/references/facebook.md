# Facebook (Meta) Algorithm Playbook

> How Facebook ranks and distributes content for a Page/brand maximizing organic reach.
> Anchored on Meta's Transparency Center, newsroom posts, and Alison/Mosseri statements; analyst and
> community observations are marked **[heuristic]**.
> **Last verified:** 2026-07.

## The one idea

Facebook is shifting from a **social graph** (content from friends/Pages you follow) to a
**recommendation/interest graph** (AI-picked content from accounts you don't follow) — the TikTok
model, stated openly as Meta's roadmap by Facebook head Tom Alison. Two consequences you must build around:

- **Organic Page reach has collapsed** to ~1–2% of followers (industry benchmarks near ~1.4% reach).
  Feed alone will not grow you.
- **Reels and Groups are the growth surfaces** — the only placements that reliably serve your content
  to **strangers** at scale.

## Surfaces — who each reaches

| Surface | Optimizes for | Reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Main Feed** | Per-user relevance score; "meaningful" interaction | Mostly **connections** + a growing slice of AI recs |
| **Reels** | **Watch time / completion**, replays, shares | **Strangers** — the primary discovery engine |
| **Stories** | Light engagement (taps, replies), recency | **Connections** — loyal-audience nurture |
| **Groups** | Member-to-member conversation | **Members + interest-matched non-members** — strong unconnected reach |
| **Video tab** | Longer watch time, retention | Mix of connected + recommended |

**Rule of thumb: Reels and Groups drive unconnected reach; Feed and Stories lean on existing connections.**

## Ranking signals

Meta's **official four-stage Feed pipeline:** Inventory → Signals ("hundreds of thousands") →
Predictions (how likely *you* are to comment/share/find it meaningful, and how likely your friends
comment if you share) → a single Relevance score; ~500 posts sorted, highest on top.

The guiding value is **Meaningful Social Interactions (MSI):** conversation *between people* —
comments, and especially **reshares with commentary** — count more than passive likes.

**Rough working priority** (official predictions + observed weighting):
1. **Predicted meaningful comments / conversation**
2. **Shares / sends — especially private sends to Messenger** (strongest signal for *new-audience*
   reach; mirrors Instagram's confirmed "sends-per-reach" driver)
3. **Watch time / video completion & retention** (the #1 Reels signal)
4. **Dwell time** (lingering, even without interacting)
5. **Likes / reactions per reach**
6. **Relationship / affinity**, then **recency/freshness** (same-day content favored on Reels)
7. **Negative signals (demote):** hide, "See less," unfollow/snooze, **report** — strong downward multipliers

## Format: what wins

- **Reels / native short video = strongest** organic reach (often 2–3× a photo post). This is where
  the AI recommendation system spends distribution.
- **Native video generally** beats any off-platform video link.
- **Images** — stable but low reach and shrinking Feed priority.
- **Text-only** — minimal reach unless it sparks a comment thread (works better inside Groups).
- **External-link posts — structurally downranked.** Meta's Widely Viewed Content Report shows
  ~98.5% of Feed content views contained **no** external link. Put links in the first comment, not the body.
- **Groups** — the reach multiplier for community/discussion content.

## First hour [heuristic]

Early velocity acts as a test batch: shown to a small slice, engagement (comments, shares, dwell,
3-sec holds) decides expansion. **Reply to comments fast in the first hour.** On Reels, the decisive
test is the **first 3 seconds** — aim for **70%+ completion** to trigger amplification. Best posting
windows (third-party studies): Tue/Wed, mornings and evenings — because they concentrate early engagement.

## Downranked / penalized (official demotion categories)

- **Engagement bait** ("Like if…", "Tag a friend", "Comment YES", "Share to win"). Repeat-offender
  **Pages get harsher, Page-wide demotion.** (Genuine calls for help/advice/fundraising are exempt.)
- **Clickbait / misleading headlines**, ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation.
- **Links to low-quality pages** (ad-choked, slow, thin).
- **Unoriginal / repurposed content** — formalized July 2025: repeat offenders lose monetization and
  get reduced distribution across everything they post; duplicate videos demoted in favor of the original.
- **Recycled / other-app-watermarked video** (e.g., visible TikTok watermark), and **AI slop**.
- **Misinformation** (fact-checked-false) and sensational/borderline content.

## Groups — the durable organic engine

Per-post reach dwarfs Pages (a 10k-member Group post often reaches 2,000–5,000+; the same 10k Page
audience sees ~200–600 — roughly a 10× advantage) **[heuristic]**, because Group posts draw
member-to-member comments (exactly the MSI signal) and Facebook both notifies members of hot posts
and suggests Group content to interest-matched non-members. Run or actively participate in a niche
Group, seed genuine discussion (not bait), post native content that invites replies.

## Judge success by

1. **Shares / sends (esp. private sends)** — best predictor of unconnected-reach expansion.
2. **Meaningful comments & reply depth** — the core MSI signal.
3. **Video retention / completion %** — the Reels amplification trigger (target 70%+).
4. **Non-follower reach %** — the real growth scoreboard.
Reactions/likes and follower count are trailing vanity indicators.

## Do this / avoid this

**Do**
- Go **Reels-first** (2–3+ native Reels/week); nail the first 3 seconds; optimize for completion + rewatch.
- Engineer **shares, especially private sends** — make content someone DMs to one friend.
- Frame for **meaningful comments** (real, specific questions); reply within the first hour.
- Post native; keep links in the comments. Lean on **Groups** for community reach.

**Avoid**
- Engagement-bait phrasing; clickbait; external-link posts as primary format.
- Reposting others' content without transformation; recycled/watermarked video; AI slop.
- Chasing volume — weak posts drag your predicted relevance. Optimizing for reactions.

## For BettorEdge specifically (edit to taste)

- **Reels-first** for reach: a 15–30s cold-open on a result or edge ("no-vig turned this $50 into
  $410"), native, watermark-free, built for completion + a private send to a betting buddy.
- **A BettorEdge Group** is likely your single most durable organic channel — a place for slate talk,
  bad-beat threads, and picks where member-to-member comments do the distribution work Pages can't.
- Keep the app link in the first comment, never the post body.
